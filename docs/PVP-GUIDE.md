# PvP & Guild Warfare Guide

This document covers competitive gameplay, guild territories, and the Ashen Lists PvP arena.

## The Ashen Lists Arena

### Overview
**Location:** Guild Hall in Emberhold  
**Level Requirement:** 40+  
**Entry:** Voluntary (no penalties for deaths)  
**Time Limit:** None (continuous matches)  
**Team Size:** 1v1, small team, or guild-scale battles

### Arena Layout

The Ashen Lists is a 44×44 tile volcanic battlefield designed for tactical guild warfare.

```
    ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
    │ N-Platform    Pillar    E-Plat  │
    │ [Banner]   [Lamp]   [Banner]    │
    │                                  │
    │  [Pillar]              [Pillar]  │
    │    NW                    NE      │
    │                                  │
    │[Lamp]  [Barrel]   [Barrel]  [Lamp]
    │  W     Central      Central    E
    │      [Lava Pit]                │
    │                                  │

    │    SW                    SE      │
    │                                  │
    │ S-Platform    Pillar    W-Plat  │
    │  [Barrel]   [Lamp]   [Barrel]   │
    │ [Banner]              [Banner]   │
    └─────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Key Features

#### Central Lava Pit (20, 18) — 4×4 tiles
- **Hazard:** Deals constant damage to standing players
- **Purpose:** Creates central no-man's land, forces tactical play
- **Strategies:**
  - Tank classes can tank pit damage as sacrificial play
  - Ranged classes use pit as cover from melee rushes
  - Jump across pit to split enemies
  - Guild can deny center map = deny power-ups

#### Tactical Pillars (8 total)
- **Cardinal Pillars:** (6,22) (36,22) (22,8) (22,34) — 2×2 tiles each
  - Arranged in cross pattern around center
  - Creates lanes of engagement
  - Each pillar holds sight line to 2-3 adjacent pillars

- **Diagonal Pillars:** (10,12) (32,12) (10,30) (32,30) — 2×2 tiles each
  - Enables flanking routes around cardinal pillars
  - Creates high-skill positional play
  - Separates initiators from back-line

**Positioning Theory:**
- **Defense:** Cluster around one pillar set (north/south), fall back to platform
- **Aggression:** Use diagonal pillars to flank, isolate enemy players
- **Control:** Hold cardinal pillars (especially north), deny southern push

#### Elevated Platforms (4 total) — 6×3 tiles each
- **North Platform** (4, 4): Overlooks northern arena and lava pit
- **Northeast Platform** (34, 4): Flanking high ground
- **Southwest Platform** (4, 37): Secondary defensive position
- **Southeast Platform** (34, 37): Opposite side flanking point

**Strategic Uses:**
- **Ranged Superiority:** Archers/mages hold platform for sight-line advantage
- **Guild Clash:** One guild secures two platforms = positional win
- **Escape Route:** Damaged players retreat to platform for healing/mana
- **Ambush Point:** Assassins drop from platform on unsuspecting enemies

#### Supply Caches (4 barrels) — Neutral Resources
- **North Cache** (22, 12): Between center and north pillar
- **South Cache** (22, 28): Between center and south pillar
- **West Cache** (12, 20): Between center and west pillar
- **East Cache** (32, 20): Between center and east pillar

**Mechanics:**
- Opened by any player (no lockout)
- Currently decorative (intended for future power-up system)
- Position incentivizes fights at strategic points (not just center)

#### Braziers & Banners (Decorative)
- **North Braziers** (2, 2) (40, 2): Mark spawn zone boundary
- **South Braziers** (2, 40) (40, 40): Mark exit zone boundary
- **Side Braziers** (2, 20) (40, 20): Mark arena edges
- **Guild Banners** (10, 6) (32, 6) (10, 38) (32, 38): Mark guild territory

## Combat Tactics

### Solo Play (1v1)

#### Ranger Strategy
1. Control northern platform (ranged advantage)
2. Kite enemy around pillars (line-of-sight breaks)
3. Use southwest platform as escape/mana recovery point
4. Never engage in lava pit (melee wins in close range)

**Counters:** Assassins can flank via diagonal pillars; Mages can burst from range.

#### Melee Strategy
1. Rush diagonals (10,12) or (32,12) to surprise enemy
2. Pin enemy against a pillar (reduces retreat options)
3. Use lava pit as terrain advantage (you know hazard, enemy panics)
4. Fall back to nearest platform if outmatched

**Counters:** Rangers keep distance; Mages kite around pillars.

#### Mage Strategy
1. Control center space (lava pit blocks melee)
2. Position at cardinal pillars for AoE coverage
3. Teleport to platform if targeted by assassins
4. Rain spells on enemies pinned by your own lava hazard

**Counters:** Assassins gap-close via diagonals; Melee outheal mage damage if repositioned well.

### Guild-Scale Warfare (6v6+)

#### North Strategy (Defensive Hold)
```
Objective: Control northern platform + cardinal north pillar

Formation:
  Ranged: North Platform (6,4) — overlooks entire arena
  Tank:   Cardinal North (22,8) — blocks push
  Healer: Northeast Platform (34,4) — supports from flank
  DPS:    Diagonal NW (10,12) — ambush incoming
  Reserve: Southwest Platform (4,37) — secondary fallback
```

**Timing:**
- Phase 1 (0-2min): Secure platforms, deny enemy north control
- Phase 2 (2-5min): Push toward cardinal pillars, isolate stragglers
- Phase 3 (5+min): Hold north half, bleed enemy resources

**Vulnerability:** Over-committed to north leaves east/west flanks open.

#### South Strategy (Offensive Push)
```
Objective: Capture south-center and flip arena

Formation:
  Tank:   Cardinal South (22,34) — spearhead push
  DPS:    Diagonal SW (10,30) — wrap around west flank
  DPS:    Diagonal SE (32,30) — wrap around east flank
  Ranged: East Platform (34,4) — provide overlapping fire
  Healer: Cardinal Center (implicit, plays the gaps)
```

**Timing:**
- Phase 1 (0-1min): Form wedge at south cardinal pillar
- Phase 2 (1-3min): Push into center lava pit area (cut map in half)
- Phase 3 (3+min): Force enemy north, compress them into platforms

**Vulnerability:** Overextended without ranged support on platforms (enemy counter-push).

#### Center Strategy (Control)
```
Objective: Control lava pit zone, split enemy team

Formation:
  Tank:    Rotate cardinal pillars (north → east → south → west)
  DPS:     Flank via diagonals while tank holds sight-line
  Ranged:  Hold platform for LOS, rain fire into cardinal positions
  Healer:  Float near two-pillar distance, support nearest tank
```

**Timing:**
- Phase 1 (0-2min): Establish cardinal pillar rotations
- Phase 2 (2-5min): Eliminate isolated enemies between pillars
- Phase 3 (5+min): Own center = own supply caches = own fights

**Vulnerability:** If enemy secures **both diagonal pillars on same side**, flanking becomes dangerous.

## Guild War Mechanics

### Declaration & Scheduling
- **War Window:** Sundays 20:00 UTC (one-hour war period)
- **Declaration:** Guild leader declares war via guild panel (optional)
- **Cost:** 500 Aurum per war (separate from siege fee)

### Fortress Siege (High-Stakes War)

#### Weekly Siege (Mondays 00:00 UTC)
- **Objective:** Capture and hold the Fortress Throne for 90 seconds
- **Reward (Winners):** No weekly maintenance cost for guild (500 Aurum saved)
- **Reward (Runners-up):** No EXP loss for week (consolation prize)
- **Duration:** One-hour window, continuous attempts

#### Who Can Siege?
- **Any guild** with 5+ members can declare intent
- **Competing guilds** fight on fortress grounds simultaneously
- **Last one standing** wins the week's protection

#### Fortress Layout
- Central throne room (60×60 tiles)
- Four gate entrances (north, south, east, west)
- 8 tower emplacements (automatic turret damage to intruders)
- Healing fountains (neutral, HP regeneration only)

#### Winning Strategy
1. **Gate Control:** Secure one entrance, create chokepoint
2. **Tower Denial:** Eliminate enemy players guarding towers (removes turret fire)
3. **Route Clearance:** Clear path to throne room from your gate
4. **Throne Push:** 6-person rush, stand on throne for 90 seconds
5. **Hold Phase:** Defend throne against incoming enemies

#### Anti-Strategy (Defender)
- If inside fortress: distribute forces to all four gates
- Sacrifice tank at chokepoint while DPS focuses enemy ranged
- Once enemy commits to one gate, push out other gates behind them
- Force them to fight on multiple fronts (tire out coordination)

## Rewards & Consequences

### PvP Rewards
- **Weekly Guild War Bonus:** +5% EXP for guild members (if guild holds siege)
- **Personal Ranking:** Informal (no official ladder, but bragging rights)
- **Cosmetics:** Future feature (team colors, guild cloaks)

### PvP Penalties
- **Death in PvP:** No EXP loss, no item drop
- **Surrender Mechanics:** If match goes 10+ minutes, team can surrender (respects time)
- **Raid Lock:** War participation does not lock you out of PvE raids

### Guild War Costs
- **Declaration Fee:** 500 Aurum per war period
- **Siege Fee:** Waived for week if you win the siege
- **Failed Siege:** Still pay 500 Aurum, lose the war
- **No Income Penalty:** Defeating players earns no direct gold (prevents farming)

## Class-Specific PvP Roles

### Warrior (Tank)
- **Role:** Frontline initiator, controls terrain
- **Tactics:** 
  - Pin enemies against pillars (reduce escape paths)
  - Body-block platforms from ranged enemies
  - Rotate between cardinal pillars to create moving targets
- **Weakness:** Low mobility in open space

### Rogue (Assassin)
- **Role:** Burst damage, isolation specialist
- **Tactics:**
  - Use diagonal pillars to approach from unexpected angles
  - Eliminate isolated ranged enemies (especially healers)
  - Chain-stun multiple targets across platform gaps
- **Weakness:** Dies to focused guild fire; needs support

### Mage (Spellcaster)
- **Role:** Area control, ranged pressure
- **Tactics:**
  - Position at cardinal pillars for AoE pillar denial
  - Use lava pit as extra hazard (enemies retreat further)
  - Teleport to platform if rushed by melee
- **Weakness:** Low HP, relies on distance and positioning

### Priest (Healer)
- **Role:** Resource preservation, team survival
- **Tactics:**
  - Float at two-pillar distance from frontline
  - Prioritize tank healing (enables longer positioning holds)
  - Use resurrection on key players (turn fights)
- **Weakness:** Targeted by assassins (make healer your second priority after tank)

### Ranger (Archer)
- **Role:** Platform control, consistent ranged damage
- **Tactics:**
  - Secure north or south platform (overlooks arena)
  - Rain arrows on enemies near cardinal pillars
  - Suppress enemy platforms with constant fire
- **Weakness:** Low burst, relies on teammate follow-up

## Etiquette & Community

### Good Sportsmanship
- **Respect Opponents:** Acknowledge good plays from enemy guilds
- **No Spawn Camping:** Don't fight at warp exit (let players rebuff)
- **Fair Play:** Report exploits (bug abuse loses you respect)
- **Competitive Spirit:** Winning is fun, but so is a good fight

### Dispute Resolution
- **Report Exploits:** Use `/report` command with evidence
- **Guild Disputes:** Guild leaders settle via `/guild arbitrate` (neutral mediator)
- **Repeated Violations:** Account-level PvP bans (escalating)

## Practice & Preparation

### Solo Duel Training
1. Enter Ashen Lists (solo option)
2. Practice positioning around each pillar set
3. Learn platform jump timings
4. Train kiting around center lava pit

### Guild Scrimmages
1. Schedule internal guild 3v3 (first team to 5 kills wins)
2. Rotate players through positions (tank, DPS, healer)
3. Debrief after each scrim: what positioning worked?
4. Film scrims (replay via client demo mode) to analyze

### Week-Before Prep
1. **Day 1-3:** Grind gear (highest refine level)
2. **Day 4-5:** Run guild dungeons (buff stones, coordination)
3. **Day 6:** Guild meeting (strategy, call-outs, role assignments)
4. **Day 7:** Rest (mental clarity for war day)

## Summary

The Ashen Lists is designed for strategic depth: controlling pillars, managing lava hazard, and denying platforms creates emergent gameplay where positioning trumps raw numbers. A well-coordinated small team can defeat a poorly-organized large one.

**Core Principle:** In PvP, **terrain is as important as gear**.
