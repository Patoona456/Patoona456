# Chibi art

`source/hero_brown.png` is the walk board supplied by the project owner (not
LPC, and not covered by the LPC licences in `assets/lpc/`).

`body/hero_brown.png` is the same art re-cut onto a grid by
`tools/slice-chibi.py`: 128x192 frames, 4 walk frames across, 8 facings down
in DIR8 order. Re-run the script after editing the source.
